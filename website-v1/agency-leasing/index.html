The Landmark, Hong Kong

Turn the retail plan into a leaseable offer

A leasing campaign works best when the commercial story, retail planning and target tenant mix point in the same direction. We connect the positioning strategy and retail programme before the first approach to the market. The strategy sets out who the destination should attract, how brands fit the positioning and which units can support their requirements. The execution then takes that offer into direct conversations with retailers.

This gives the project team a practical basis for:

  • Prioritising anchors, categories and new-to-market brands
  • Presenting the destination to regional and international decision-makers
  • Managing site visits, proposals and commercial discussions
  • Moving agreed terms through documentation and signature

When to appoint an agency leasing partner

Developers often bring us in before opening, when the positioning and layout still need to translate into a credible leasing programme. We can also support an operating destination that needs a stronger mix, lease renewals or a new approach to underperforming space.

A leasing appointment may begin when:

  • A new development needs a tenant strategy and route to market
  • A landlord needs direct access to regional or global brand teams
  • The current mix no longer supports the intended positioning
  • Renewals, conversions or vacant units require coordinated negotiation

One mandate from strategy through execution

Our role follows the agreed mandate. For a landlord or developer, this may mean agency leasing and landlord representation across the project. For a retailer, it may mean tenant representation, market entry advice and support through location and lease decisions. We define the side we represent and the decisions we own at the start of the appointment.

What our agency leasing service delivers

Each project enters the market with different constraints. Our leasing services can combine five connected workstreams.

01 Retail mix and leasing strategy

We turn the destination's positioning strategy into category, anchor and tenant priorities. The work can cover tenant mix, broad zoning, anchor strategy, target brands, unit requirements and the sequence of approaches. Market and competition evidence helps test the proposed offer before the leasing campaign advances.

02 Leasing marketing tools

Retailers need a clear reason to join the project. We help shape the materials used in that discussion, including brand identity decks, show suites and roadshow presentations. The content connects the customer proposition, location, design and commercial opportunity in one account of the destination.

03 Tenant introductions and market engagement

We introduce the project to relevant brand leaders and leasing teams, drawing on regional and international relationships. Meetings, roadshows and site visits give retailers the information needed to assess the opportunity. Their feedback can also identify gaps in the mix or changes needed at unit level.

04 Negotiation through signature

We support commercial discussions from the first proposal through agreed terms. The work can include letters of intent, tenancy templates and coordination with the client's legal and project teams. Clear records and approval points keep negotiations moving without separating the commercial promise from the signed lease.

05 Renewals and lease conversions

Leasing continues after opening. We can support renewals, conversions and discussions with existing tenants as the destination develops. This allows the landlord to review individual leases in the context of the wider tenant mix, rather than treating each expiry as a separate transaction.

Agency leasing in practice

Our project work spans mixed-use destinations, integrated resorts and premium outlet villages. The examples below show how strategy, brand access and transaction support connect.

Elements, Hong Kong

For MTR Corporation, our pre-opening role covered prospective tenant identification and introductions, marketing and communications advice, international meetings, roadshows and lease negotiations. The brief aimed to secure new-to-Hong Kong fashion and lifestyle brands. The 82,700 sq m destination brought brands including Asia's first H&M, Damiani, Karen Millen and Megu into the scheme, alongside interest from major luxury houses.

Galaxy Macau Phase 2

Galaxy Entertainment appointed Husband Retail as exclusive leasing agent for the 55,000 sq m Phase 2 retail mall. The work connected competition analysis and customer profiling with positioning, tenant mix, introductions and lease negotiation. Phase 2 was fully leased by its 2015 opening, including a fully leased luxury zone and Macau's first Apple store. We were later re-engaged for retail advisory on Phase 4.

Suzhou Village and Shanghai Village

As leading leasing partner for Value Retail's first two premium outlet villages in China, we developed the retail mix and leasing strategy, opened access to regional and Chinese brand heads, arranged presentations and site visits, and negotiated leases through signature. Suzhou Village opened in October 2014 and Shanghai Village in May 2016 with stores trading at each grand opening. The work continued through Phase 2 leasing, renewals and conversions.

From target list to completed tenancy

Our leasing process follows the commercial decisions that need to be made, rather than a fixed sequence applied to every scheme.

  1. 01 Define the mandate and leasing objectives

    We review the project stage, positioning, plans, commercial assumptions and existing leasing activity. This establishes the scope, target outcomes, decision owners and reporting route.

  2. 02 Build the leasing strategy

    We set the priority categories, anchors, target tenants and approach sequence. The strategy also identifies the materials and project information needed for credible retailer conversations.

  3. 03 Take the offer to market

    We contact relevant decision-makers, present the opportunity and coordinate meetings, roadshows or site visits. Feedback is recorded against the target list and brought back to the project team.

  4. 04 Negotiate and document agreements

    We support proposals and commercial negotiations through LOI and tenancy documentation, working with the client's legal, finance and project teams. Progress reporting keeps decisions visible across the campaign.

  5. 05 Support opening and the next leasing cycle

    Where the mandate continues, we help move tenants towards store opening and support later renewals or lease conversions. This carries the original tenant-mix logic into the operating destination.

Questions about leasing strategy and execution

What does agency leasing include

Agency leasing can cover positioning the opportunity, setting the tenant mix, preparing leasing materials, identifying prospects, arranging introductions and site visits, negotiating terms and supporting documentation through signature. The exact scope depends on the landlord's team and the stage of the project.

When should leasing strategy begin

Leasing strategy should begin early enough to influence the retail plan and unit configuration. Retailer requirements can affect sizes, adjacencies, services and opening sequence. Design review can test whether the plans support the intended tenant mix and the balance of retail, dining and entertainment (RDE). Early market contact can test assumptions before commercial decisions become harder to change.

Can Husband Retail act as exclusive leasing partner

Yes. Our project record includes appointments as exclusive leasing agent and joint exclusive leasing partner. The mandate, market coverage, responsibilities and approval process are agreed for each project.

Do you provide tenant representation

We can support retailers and occupiers with market entry, location strategy, property assessment and lease discussions. This is separate from landlord representation. We confirm the represented party and any conflict boundaries before starting the assignment.

Can you support lease renewals or restructuring

Yes. The appointment can include renewals, lease conversions and discussions on existing tenancy terms. We assess each case against the wider leasing strategy and tenant mix, then support the agreed negotiation route.

Does your work cover luxury retail leasing

Yes. Our track record includes luxury-positioned destinations and direct engagement with international luxury brands. The recommended brand list still starts with the destination, customer and market opportunity; luxury is included where it supports the positioning and commercial plan.
