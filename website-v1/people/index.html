Our people

Retail experience across property and investment

Our retail real estate consultants work with developers, landlords and investors on the decisions that shape retail destinations. We bring together retail planning, leasing and investment experience to connect the proposed offer with the property’s commercial objectives. Meet the people whose experience informs that advice.

Our leadership

Paul Husband

Managing Partner

Paul’s career spans retail real estate in Asia Pacific and the Middle East. Before launching Husband Retail, he spent a decade heading marketing at Swire Properties.

His project experience includes Elements in Hong Kong, Galaxy Macau and DLF Emporio in New Delhi. His work has covered destination planning and retailer representation, with assignments for brands including Ralph Lauren and Chrome Hearts. He is co-author of The Cult of the Luxury Brand, a study of luxury consumption in Asia.

Jasmine Wong

Managing Partner

Jasmine brings experience in real estate investment, asset management and advisory across Asia Pacific. Her career includes roles at LaSalle Investment Management, Pamfleet and the Abu Dhabi Investment Authority.

Her experience covers sourcing and structuring investments, joint ventures and asset management through to disposal. At Husband Retail, her focus spans the consulting and leasing teams, asset management and strategic advisory. This brings an investment perspective to the assessment of retail opportunities and the management of property performance.
