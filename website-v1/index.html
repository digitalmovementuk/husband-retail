The firm

A single focus on retail real estate

A retail destination needs a clear role in its market. Customer demand, the mix of brands and the way people move through a place all influence that role.

We bring these decisions together through research, retail planning and leasing advice. Our work spans shopping centres, mixed-use developments, integrated resorts and premium outlet villages, from early feasibility studies to the repositioning of operating destinations.

Our project experience extends from Hong Kong and mainland China to other Asian markets, the Middle East and London.
