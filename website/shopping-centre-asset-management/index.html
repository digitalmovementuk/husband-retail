Assess the retail assumptions behind the investment

A retail business plan rests on assumptions about who will visit, what they will spend and which tenants will trade successfully. Those assumptions need a clear market basis. Our investment advisory examines the retail proposition through market analysis, consumer research and review of the business plan. The findings help the owner assess the proposed scale and positioning before committing to the next stage.

This gives the investment and asset team a clearer basis for deciding:

  • Which customer groups the destination can serve
  • Whether the proposed retail scale reflects market demand
  • How the tenant mix supports the positioning strategy
  • Which planning assumptions need further review

When to bring in retail investment advice

A tender, a proposed retail property acquisition or a review of an existing asset can raise questions about the commercial plan. Early advice helps the team examine those questions before the retail concept and development commitments become harder to change.

The starting point may be a new opportunity or a change in the property’s market:

  • Does the business plan reflect how customers will use the destination?
  • Is the proposed retail offer distinct from nearby competitors?
  • Would a different scale or phasing better suit demand?
  • Does an existing asset need a revised positioning or tenant mix?

What our investment advisory covers

The scope starts with the decision the client needs to make. Our documented investment advisory work brings together four areas.

01 Tender document review

We review the tender documents from a retail perspective, examining how the requirements relate to the proposed destination. This helps frame the questions that the business plan and supporting research need to address.

02 Business plan review

We examine the retail proposition within the business plan and provide feedback on planning and positioning. The review connects the proposed offer with the market evidence supporting its financial viability.

03 Market analysis and consumer research

Market analysis and consumer research examine demand for the proposed retail, dining and entertainment (RDE) offer. They give the team evidence to assess the destination’s role, intended customers and relationship with competing locations.

04 Planning and positioning advice

Research findings inform recommendations on the retail concept, scale and positioning. Where relevant to the brief, advice on phasing helps the developer consider how individual stages can form a coherent destination.

From the investment question to retail recommendations

An appointment can be organised around four stages, with the scope and outputs agreed for the project.

  1. 01 Define the decision

    Establish the property, investment objectives and questions to resolve. Identify the available tender documents, business plan and retail information that will inform the review.

  2. 02 Examine the market

    Assess customer demand and the competitive context. Use the findings to examine the retail assumptions behind the proposed offer and scale.

  3. 03 Review the proposition

    Bring the market findings into the business plan review. Examine how positioning, planning and phasing relate to the opportunity the team is assessing.

  4. 04 Set out the implications

    Present retail recommendations for the client’s next decisions. Agree where further research, development consultancy or leasing advice is needed to take the proposition forward.

Connect asset strategy with the retail offer

Shopping centre asset management needs a clear view of the customers the property serves. Our retail strategy work can inform that view through retail planning, tenant mix and an anchor strategy linked to the intended positioning.

Where a wider appointment covers development or repositioning, these recommendations can extend into a retail programme, broad zoning and design review. New-to-market brands may form part of the proposed offer where they fit the destination and its customers. The scope connects each recommendation with a defined property decision.

Questions about investment advisory and asset management

How does retail strategy support shopping centre asset management?

Retail strategy links the owner’s objectives with customer demand, positioning and tenant mix. It helps an asset team assess whether the offer remains relevant and identify where research, repositioning or leasing advice could support its plans.

What does retail investment due diligence cover?

Our documented tender due diligence includes review of tender documents and the business plan, supported by market analysis and consumer research. Planning and positioning feedback helps the client assess the retail assumptions behind the proposal. The exact scope follows the appointment.

Can you help assess a retail property acquisition?

A retail property acquisition raises questions about demand, positioning and the proposed offer. We can discuss a retail advisory brief around those questions. The required work and its relationship with the buyer’s other due diligence should be agreed at the outset.

Does retail asset management include daily property operations?

This service focuses on strategic retail advice for investment and asset decisions. Retail property asset management can involve several disciplines, so responsibilities for daily operations, facilities and property management need to be established separately within the client’s team.

Can the advice lead into leasing execution?

Yes. Our Leasing Strategy & Execution service can take the retail recommendations into tenant discussions and negotiations under an agreed mandate. Husband Retail undertakes appointments as exclusive leasing partner, with the role and responsibilities defined for each project.

Can the review confirm future investment returns?

The review provides a retail market basis for assessing the proposal. Financial outcomes depend on the assumptions adopted, subsequent execution and trading conditions. The client can use the findings alongside its financial and other professional advice when making the investment decision.
