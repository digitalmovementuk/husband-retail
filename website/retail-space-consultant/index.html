What the appointment covers

A retail space consultant decides what goes where, and why.

The phrase covers a narrow job: reading a trade area, then turning floor plates into a merchandising plan that trades. Which units carry which categories. Where the anchors sit. What the circulation has to do to pull footfall past the rents that pay for the scheme.

We have done that work on more than 100 schemes since 1998, and then leased most of them ourselves.

  • Trade area and catchment analysis
  • Spend capture and competing supply
  • Zoning, adjacencies and unit strategy
  • Anchor and category planning

When to appoint one

Before the drawings harden, not after.

The cheapest time to move a wall is on paper. Most of the value a retail space consultant adds is spent in the months before the design is frozen, when unit sizes, servicing and vertical circulation can still change.

We are also called in later, to a centre already trading below its catchment. That is repositioning, and it starts the same way: an honest read of the trade area and the existing mix.

  • Site acquired, concept not yet fixed
  • Master plan in progress
  • Scheme under construction, leasing not started
  • Open and trading below catchment

The work itself

What a retail space consultant actually delivers.

Four documents, in this order. Each one is only as good as the study underneath it.

01

Feasibility study

Trade area, catchment, competing supply and spend capture. The honest read of whether the scheme stacks up at the GLA proposed, and at what rent.

02

Concept and positioning

Who the centre is for, what it sells, and what it must feel like to beat the alternatives already open in that catchment.

03

Merchandising plan

The zoning document: categories to floors, adjacencies, anchor placement, unit sizes and the circulation those decisions depend on.

04

Leasing strategy

The target brand list, the order to approach it in, and the deal terms the plan can carry. Then the negotiation itself, heads of terms to signature.

A plan nobody signs is just paper.

Why a boutique

Why the size of the firm changes the advice.

Someone who has negotiated heads of terms with a luxury brand’s head of real estate writes a very different merchandising plan from someone who has only modelled one. That combination — planning and leasing in the same hands — is rarer than it sounds.

We define the side we represent and the decisions we own at the start of the appointment.

It also keeps us small. We take few mandates and give each the founder’s time. If you want your asset treated as one file among hundreds, we are the wrong appointment.

Questions

Questions principals ask.

What does a retail space consultant do that an architect does not?

The architect designs the building. We decide what the building has to contain to trade: the categories, the unit sizes, the anchors and the adjacencies. The two disciplines have to argue with each other early, on paper, before the drawings harden.

Do you act for owners or for occupiers?

We can support retailers and occupiers with market entry, location strategy, property assessment and lease discussions. This is separate from landlord representation. We confirm the represented party and any conflict boundaries before starting the assignment.

How early should we appoint you?

Before the concept design is frozen. Most of the value is in decisions about unit sizes, servicing and circulation, and those get expensive to change once they are drawn.

What if the feasibility work says the scheme does not stack up?

We tell you, in writing, before you commit capex. It has happened. When the answer is no, we set out what would have to change for it to become yes.

Can you bring Louis Vuitton or Hermès into our asset?

We have recruited both, along with Chanel, Cartier, Prada and others, into centres we planned. No adviser can commit a named brand. We can shape the asset those brands ask for, then make the case to people we have dealt with for years.

Which markets do you cover?

More than 20 markets across Asia, the Middle East and Europe, from Hong Kong and Seoul to Doha, Dubai and London. We are based in Hong Kong and travel to the asset.
