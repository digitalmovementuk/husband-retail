Most malls collect the data. Few use it to decide.

AI helps us find patterns faster. These four steps show how that becomes a decision you can act on.

Your data

Start with the data you have

Most malls already collect useful data: footfall counts, tenant sales, dwell times, car park use and sometimes loyalty or Wi-Fi data. It is often spread across different systems and rarely looked at together.

We bring those sources into one picture, check their quality and agree with you what can and cannot be used. [CONFIRM: data sources and tools used]

Analysis

Patterns that are easy to miss

AI tools help us spot patterns that would take weeks to find by hand: which zones lose shoppers, which tenants draw people further into the mall, and how trading shifts by hour, day and season.

Each finding is tested against what we know about retail before it becomes a recommendation.

Decisions

From dashboard to leasing decision

A chart on its own does not change a mall. We translate the findings into specific actions: which units to re-tenant first, where a new anchor would help most, how to adjust the mix on a quieter floor.

Each recommendation comes with the evidence behind it and the result we would expect.

Judgement

Retail experience in the loop

Data shows what is happening; experience explains why. Our team has worked on more than 100 retail projects across Asia Pacific and the Middle East, and that experience checks every conclusion the models suggest.

The aim is better decisions, made faster, with people who understand retail accountable for them.

Questions

Frequently asked questions

What is AI-driven shopping mall consultancy and how does it work?

It is mall consultancy that uses AI tools to analyse a mall's own data, such as footfall, tenant sales and dwell time, alongside market data. The analysis points to concrete decisions on tenant mix, leasing and layout, which our retail team then checks and turns into a plan.

What data does our mall need to share with you?

Usually footfall counts, tenant sales where leases allow it, the tenant list and lease expiries, and any visitor or car park data you already collect. We start with what you have and tell you which gaps are worth closing. [CONFIRM: minimum data set]

Does AI replace the leasing team at our mall?

No. AI speeds up the analysis and finds patterns, but leasing still depends on relationships, negotiation and judgement. Experienced retail people stay in charge of every recommendation and every conversation with tenants.

How is our mall's footfall and tenant data handled?

Your data is used only for your project and handled under a written agreement that sets out storage, access and deletion. Tenant sales data is treated as confidential. We agree these terms with you before any data is shared. [CONFIRM: data handling terms]

Does AI-driven mall consultancy work for smaller malls?

Yes. Smaller malls and retail podiums often have less data, but even basic footfall and sales figures can show where the tenant mix or layout is holding the property back. We scale the analysis to the data and the decisions at hand.
