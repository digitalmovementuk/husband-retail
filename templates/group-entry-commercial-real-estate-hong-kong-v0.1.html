Commercial property in Hong Kong, seen through a retail lens.

Hong Kong's commercial market is wide. Our part of it is retail, and these four points explain what that means for you.

Retail focus

Commercial property, seen through retail

Many commercial real estate firms in Hong Kong cover offices, industrial and retail together. We concentrate on the retail side: the shopping malls, retail podiums and shops that depend on footfall, tenant mix and the right brands.

That focus lets us go deeper on the questions that decide how retail property performs.

Shops and units

Shops, podiums and mall space

Whether you own a single street shop, the retail podium of a mixed-use tower or a full shopping mall, the same questions come up: who should the tenants be, what rent is realistic and how long will leasing take?

We answer them with evidence from the local market and from more than two decades of retail leasing work.

The market

Know the Hong Kong market

Hong Kong rents and demand can change sharply from one district to the next, and from one side of a street to the other. We follow the retail market closely, including tenants moving between districts and the effect of new transport links.

Owners and investors use this knowledge to time leasing, set rents and plan upgrades.

Questions

Frequently asked questions

Do you handle all types of commercial real estate in Hong Kong?

Our work centres on retail: shopping malls, retail podiums, street shops and the retail parts of mixed-use and transit-oriented developments. If your question is about offices or industrial space with no retail element, we will tell you early and, where we can, suggest where to turn. [CONFIRM: scope outside retail]

Can you help me lease a commercial property in Hong Kong?

Yes, if the property is retail or has a retail element. Our agency leasing team markets the space, finds and qualifies tenants and negotiates terms for landlords. We also act for retailers and brands that are looking for space.

Do you advise investors on commercial property in Hong Kong?

Yes. We advise investors on retail property, including how a mall or retail podium is performing, its potential and the market around it. Our advice focuses on the retail side of a property rather than general valuation. [CONFIRM: investor advisory scope]

Which parts of Hong Kong does your commercial property team cover?

We cover Hong Kong Island, Kowloon and the New Territories from our office in Wanchai. Over more than two decades we have also worked on more than 100 retail projects across Asia Pacific and the Middle East. [CONFIRM: district coverage]
