Overview

Agency leasing for new and mature destinations

Our agency leasing takes a destination from its positioning to concluded tenancies. The work differs for a scheme ahead of opening and for one already trading.

New destinations
Ahead of opening, leasing work sets the tenant mix and the brands to target, then takes the opportunity to retailers.
Established destinations

Focus areas

Leasing decisions we advise on

Where the proposition, customer demand, retailer requirements and physical plan do not support one another, we review a different tenant, configuration or location with the client.

Positioning and proposition
Positioning statements setting out the proposition for customers and prospective tenants.
Tenant mix and zoning
Mix matrices that set category balance, adjacencies, broad zoning and the role of anchors.
Unit configuration
Annotated plans of units and zoning, reviewed with the design team for leasing flexibility.
Operating requirements
A check of service access and operating needs for retail and F&B tenants.
Retail, dining and entertainment
A review of how each proposed concept or brand changes that balance.
Leasing marketing tools
Brand identity decks, show suites and roadshow presentations for prospective tenants.
Priorities and next steps
A roadmap of priorities, responsibilities and the next leasing decisions.

Where appointed, the work also covers letters of intent, tenancy templates, negotiation to signature, renewals and lease conversions. Leasing activity does not imply a guaranteed letting, and architectural and engineering responsibility stays with the specialists the client appoints.

Our approach

From leasing brief to signed agreement

Each stage ends in a deliverable the client can review. Timing and sequence vary with the brief, the evidence available and the project stage.

  1. 01

    Stage 1: Brief and priorities

    We agree the commercial objectives, decisions, scope and responsibilities with the client, including the boundaries of any leasing role.

    Deliverable:
    Agreed leasing brief
  2. 02

    Stage 2: Evidence base

    We review customer, market and retailer information alongside the proposition and physical plan, and identify the assumptions that need testing.

    Deliverable:
    Research findings
  3. 03

    Stage 3: Tenant strategy

    With the client, we agree the tenant mix, any new retail concepts and the brands to prioritise for the destination.

    Deliverable:
    Target-brand recommendations
  4. 04

    Stage 4: Engagement and negotiation

    For leasing mandates, we introduce retailers through roadshows and site visits and support letters of intent to signature with the client’s legal team.

    Deliverable:
    Tenancy documentation

FAQ

Common questions on agency leasing

Can Husband Retail act as exclusive leasing partner?

Our project record includes exclusive leasing appointments. Exclusivity, market coverage, responsibilities and approval processes depend on the agreed mandate; they are not assumed for every project.

Does strategy advice include retailer engagement?

Brand engagement and leasing are included only where appointed. A strategy brief can conclude with recommendations and agreed next steps, with any subsequent engagement defined separately.

Which developments does Husband Retail lease?

We handle leasing only for developments we have worked on. Our leasing record includes Elements in Hong Kong, Galaxy Macau Phase 2, Suzhou Village and Shanghai Village, Merdeka 118 and DLF Emporio.
