Services

We advise from first plan to signed lease

The starting point may be a new site, an emerging design or a destination ready for change. Our scope follows the decisions your team needs to make.

01

Development Consultancy & Retail Planning

Why
The scale, offer and layout need a clear basis in market demand.
How
Market research, positioning and retail planning, with design reviews alongside the architect.
What
A retail programme covering tenant mix, anchor strategy and broad zoning.
Explore development consultancy →
02

Destination Strategy & Repositioning

Why
Customer expectations and competition change the role a destination can play.
How
Research into the market and existing offer, followed by a positioning strategy.
What
Recommendations for the retail and dining mix, layout and marketing programme.
Explore destination strategy →
03

Leasing Strategy & Execution

Why
The brands in a destination need to fit its customers and positioning.
How
Leasing strategy, tenant introductions and negotiations, including appointments as exclusive leasing partner.
What
A target tenant mix, leasing materials and support through to signed agreements.
Explore leasing strategy →
04

Research & Market Intelligence

Why
Decisions about retail space need evidence of customer and retailer demand.
How
Market analysis, consumer research, brand interviews and competitor studies.
What
Findings that inform the scale, offer and positioning of the destination.
Explore research and market intelligence →
05

Marketing & Placemaking

Why
A destination’s identity, environment and customer programme shape how people experience the offer.
How
Positioning statements, marketing plans, communications, activation and tenant partnerships connected to the retail plan.
What
A marketing and placemaking programme aligned with the destination’s positioning and tenant mix.
Explore marketing and placemaking →
